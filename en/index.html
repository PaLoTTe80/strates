How to play

A layer slides back and forth above your tower. Tap the screen to drop it: anything hanging over is sliced off and falls. Your tower gets thinner with every slip, and the game ends when you miss completely.

Drop a layer exactly on the previous one for a Perfect. From three perfects in a row, the tower widens a little again: it's the only way to win back some room. The higher you climb, the faster the layers move, and the sky turns from dusk to a starry night.

  • Play: a different tower every game, to beat your best score.
  • Daily Challenge: the same tower for everyone, every day. Only your first try counts on the daily leaderboard; the next ones are practice.
  • Goals: 18 personal challenges to unlock, from “10 layers in one game” to “25 perfect drops in a row”.

Frequently asked questions

The game doesn't vibrate.

Make sure system haptics are on in Settings › Sounds & Haptics › System Haptics.

When does the daily challenge change?

The challenge follows your iPhone's date and changes at midnight. The daily Game Center leaderboard is reset every day by Apple.

I missed my official try, can I play again?

Yes, as much as you like, but as practice: only your first try of the day is sent to the daily leaderboard. Practice games still count for your best score and your goals.

How do I reset my best score and goals?

They are only stored on your iPhone. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets them.

I don't show up on the leaderboard.

Leaderboards use Game Center: sign in under Settings › Game Center, then play a game. Your score is sent at the end of each game.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not to play: Strates works offline. You only need one to send your score and view the leaderboards.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No, none.

Privacy

Strates does not collect any personal data. The game requires no account, shows no ads and uses no analytics. The developer has no server and receives no data.

On your device: your best score, game statistics, daily challenge streak and unlocked goals are stored locally. They are deleted if you remove the app.

Game Center leaderboards: if you are signed in to Game Center, your score is sent to Apple at the end of each game, to the Best leaderboard and, for your official daily try, to the Daily Challenge leaderboard. Your Game Center nickname and score are then visible to other players on these leaderboards. This data is handled by Apple under its privacy policy. To opt out, simply don't sign in to Game Center; you can also manage your visibility in Settings › Game Center.

For any privacy question, write to contact@stelmo.fr.
